# Statutory source review

These sources are references for the administrator-approved effective-dated schedule records. No rate is seeded from an unverified assumption.

| Schedule | Official source | Review status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SSS | https://www.sss.gov.ph/sss-contribution-table/ | Official page states the displayed employer and employee tables are effective January 2025. The applicable table image must be reviewed and entered for the payroll period. |
| PhilHealth | https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/partners/employers/ | Official employer page links the Premium Contribution Table. The applicable table must be reviewed and entered. |
| Pag-IBIG | https://www.pagibigfund.gov.ph/ | Use the current official contribution guidance applicable to the employer and employee classification. |
| Withholding tax | https://bir.gov.ph/ | Use current BIR withholding-tax tables and applicable employee tax status. |

Before approving production schedules, record the source circular or table reference, effective date, calculation parameters, and approving administrator in `statutory_rate_schedules`.
